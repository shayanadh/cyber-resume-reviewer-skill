# Federal, contractor, and cleared IT/cyber roles

Use the actual announcement and current official instructions. Federal employee applications and contractor resumes are not the same workflow.

## Federal resume checkpoint

Checked 2026-09-07: USAJOBS states a maximum of **two pages**, and instructs applicants to include employer, job title, month/year dates, hours per week, relevant experience, and series/grade for federal roles. Include required education/credential information and supporting documents as the announcement specifies. Recheck the live announcement and [USAJOBS resume guidance](https://help.usajobs.gov/faq/application/documents/resume/what-to-include) when preparing an application. Do not default to the old long federal resume format or hide overflow in attachments contrary to instructions.

Map specialized experience explicitly at the required level. Do not infer qualifying grade/series, veteran preference, appointment eligibility, or work hours from a title. Ask for missing mandatory details while drafting the supported portions. Do not carry federal-only fields into every commercial resume.

## Clearance and suitability

Public Trust is a suitability/position-vetting concept, not a Secret/Top Secret national-security clearance. Preserve the candidate's exact confirmed status and the job's wording; keep eligibility, current access, prior access, and willingness to undergo screening distinct. Do not certify clearance currency, reinstatement eligibility, SCI access, or polygraph status yourself. Candidate clarification from the appropriate security office is the right next step for ambiguity.

Use only job-relevant, approved status wording. Avoid publishing investigation details, identifiers, classified duties, sensitive systems, or claims that restricted work can be discussed with any interviewer. See [DCSA case types](https://www.dcsa.mil/Personnel-Vetting/Background-Investigations-for-Security-HR-Professionals/Start-a-Background-Investigation/Case-Types-Forms/) for the distinction between suitability and national-security position categories.

## DoD 8140 / DCWF

Do not use legacy “8570-compliant” shorthand as a universal qualification conclusion. Determine the position's work role, proficiency, required timing, and actual qualifying route. Certification can be one option alongside relevant education/training in the applicable qualification structure; a credential alone does not establish every qualification requirement.

Consult the [DoD qualification matrices](https://www.cyber.mil/dod-workforce-innovation-directorate/dod8140/qualification-matrices) and the current announcement/contract requirements. The official [matrix operating procedure](https://dl.dod.cyber.mil/wp-content/uploads/8140/pdf/unclass-dod-8140_matrix_sop.pdf) explains the role-aligned repository. Treat historical documents as background; do not assert a current certificate-to-role mapping without checking it.
