# Career transitions without overtranslation

Translate the work actually described; do not add plausible tasks just because someone with that background might have performed them. Preserve level in transferable capabilities while clearly identifying new-domain gaps. A domain pivot does not automatically require restarting as a junior.

| Source background | Transferable evidence to look for | Unsupported leap to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Service desk / sysadmin | Troubleshooting, account lifecycle, patching, recovery, documentation, escalation | Calling routine administration incident response or inventing hardening frameworks |
| Network engineering | Traffic analysis, segmentation, routing, availability, change validation | Claiming adversary emulation or security architecture solely from firewall use |
| Software engineering | Code, testing, design, CI/CD, reliability | Adding SAST/SCA, OAuth, threat modeling, or secure coding work not stated |
| Audit / risk | Control testing, evidence review, findings, risk communication | Turning readiness support into audit leadership or an attestation opinion |
| Intelligence / investigations | Analytical method, source evaluation, uncertainty, reporting | Inventing digital forensics, classified access, or CISO briefings |
| Military / government | Actual function, personnel responsibility, operational constraints, technical systems | Translating rank directly to VP or claiming clearance reactivation eligibility |
| Non-IT domain | Relevant analytical, regulated, service, teaching, project or management work | Claiming cyber execution from interest or enrollment alone |
| Management to IC | Relevant technical depth, recent hands-on work, desired direction | Concealing historical titles or assuming lack of commitment |
| Return to work | Relevant experience and current evidence where supplied | Demanding health/family details or treating a gap as a trust defect |

## Translation example

Source: “NCOIC for a network support team of eight; coordinated network maintenance across three sites.”

Supported: “Led an eight-person network support team and coordinated maintenance across three sites.” Keep the official title in the role header with a functional explanation if helpful. Do not add cyber defense, budgets, incident leadership, or civilian title equivalence.

Source: “Maintained backups.”

Supported: “Maintained system backups.” Ask whether the candidate tested restores, managed recovery objectives, or improved a process before adding those claims.

## Bridge narrative

A clear target headline and a short bridge can explain the move: “Systems administrator targeting security engineering, with experience in [established relevant work].” Do not relabel every prior job as cybersecurity or count all IT tenure as security tenure.

Show existing security-relevant work first. If a gap is confirmed, suggest one bounded project producing an inspectable artifact: a restore test record, a detection lab, an access-review design, a threat model, or a sample risk memo. Label it personal/lab work. A project need not be public or purchased from a training platform. Certifications are optional unless the actual target requires one; use current issuer information.

For military and cleared work, use the federal reference and only approved public descriptions. Never promise to reveal classified specifics later in an interview.
