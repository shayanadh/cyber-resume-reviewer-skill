# IT and security leadership resumes

Calibrate by actual mandate, operating context, and decision rights. Titles and headcounts vary across organizations. Do not prescribe a director, VP, or CISO team-size range or imply that a small-company leader has no transferable experience.

## Establish scope

Surface the functions accountable to the candidate, direct and indirect team relationships, geographic/business reach, reporting relationships, owned or influenced spend, critical services, and key constraints. Use supplied numbers when appropriate; qualitative scope can be credible when amounts are confidential. Distinguish budget ownership, approval authority, influence, and a project business case.

## Different leadership roles

| Role | Evidence to seek |
|---|---|
| Manager / director | Priorities, service delivery, hiring/coaching, delegation, cross-team improvements, resource decisions |
| VP / operational CISO / head of IT | Strategy implemented, operating model, business tradeoffs, accountable functions, executive governance, incident and continuity leadership |
| BISO / business-aligned leader | Business-unit constraints, influence, translated risk decisions, adoption and exception handling |
| Field CISO | Actual vendor/advisory mandate, customer problems, adopted guidance, product or commercial contribution where substantiated |
| Fractional / vCISO | Contracted responsibility, client operating role, engagement outcomes, governance; may include genuine operational accountability |
| Staff / principal IC with leadership | Technical direction, reusable systems, mentoring, cross-team influence; do not force management framing |

## Evidence distinctions

“Prepared board materials” does not mean “presented to the board.” “Advised the CFO” does not mean “approved investment.” “Oversaw response” does not mean “performed forensics.” “Built the VM function” does not mean “built all enterprise security.” Keep the actual contribution.

For richer achievements, seek a decision, constraint, action, and observed result: changed sourcing model, negotiated a tradeoff, obtained funding, developed successors, improved remediation accountability, or aligned service recovery requirements. Do not add financial loss avoided without a defensible basis.

## What to keep

Technical and offensive-security depth can distinguish an executive, especially for a hands-on mandate. Retain examples that demonstrate judgment or help the target audience; do not dismiss technical certifications as immature. Equally, do not let detailed administration tasks hide organizational responsibility.

Public speaking, teaching, publications, community service, executive education, and board work are optional relevant evidence. Lack of these is not a leadership deficiency. Executive education is not a degree, and board visibility is not board membership. Retain the issuer's actual program title and completion status.

## Length and chronology

Use the shortest document that preserves relevant scope and outcomes. Two pages can be a useful commercial starting point; target instructions and actual content decide. No automatic three-page entitlement, fixed age-based cutoff, or demand to disclose reasons for short tenure. Explain contract/fractional/concurrent roles when true. Federal applications follow their own current instructions.
