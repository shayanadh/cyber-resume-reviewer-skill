# Sources and refresh rules

Use the resume and candidate statements for their experience; the actual JD for employer requirements; authoritative external sources for current rules and technical facts. Never infer candidate experience from an employer's website or from the reviewer's personal context.

Browse only where external evidence would materially change the review: a referenced live JD, credential recommendations, federal instructions, product renaming, parser claims, or an uncertain technical point. Do not research a candidate's private history by default. Do not send their resume text or contact details to search engines or external scoring sites.

Record a source's title, URL, checked date, and the specific claim it supports. Link citations near time-sensitive claims. Prefer issuer documentation, government pages, standards owners, and ATS vendor documentation; avoid resume-SEO claims and unsupported recruiter timing statistics. Vendor statements describe that vendor and workflow, not all ATS products.

A check date is a snapshot, not an indefinite guarantee. Recheck material current rules when using the skill. If a page is inaccessible, do not pretend its current content was inspected; use an accessible primary document with its date, or state the unresolved point. Continue editing known resume content without holding it hostage to optional research.
