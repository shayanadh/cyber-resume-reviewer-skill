# Requirement triage

Preserve the employer's language before evaluating it. Importance and candidate evidence are separate axes.

| Importance | Use when |
|---|---|
| Mandatory | The announcement explicitly requires it; record whether screening enforcement is confirmed or unknown |
| Core responsibility | A central task or outcome of the job |
| Preferred | Explicitly desirable, optional, or a bonus |
| Inferred | Reviewer expectation for a title-only review; not an employer requirement |
| Unclear | Conflicting, ambiguous, or incomplete wording |

Candidate evidence statuses: **Demonstrated**, **Claimed only**, **Adjacent**, **Not shown**, **Confirmed unmet**, **Unknown**. Use Not shown for absent resume evidence, Confirmed unmet only when the candidate has established they lack it.

## Preserve Boolean meaning and timing

- “AWS or Azure” is one alternative requirement; do not require both.
- “Degree or equivalent experience” is not a degree-only gate.
- “Certification within six months of hire” is not a current-credential requirement.
- “Must hold active clearance” differs from “must be able to obtain.” Do not infer either status.
- Five years in “security or cloud engineering” can span the qualifying categories without double-counting overlapping dates. Do not claim exact tenure from year-only ranges.
- Do not silently downgrade “CISSP required” to preferred because another employer might waive it. Flag the actual wording and ask whether alternatives are accepted.

## Separate editorial effort from application eligibility

A preferred item may receive little resume space while still being relevant. Avoid the “safe to ignore” category; say “Do not delay applying solely for this preferred item” when supported. A broad or ambitious JD is not automatically noise. Identify its central outcomes and flag genuine contradictions. Do not use a 60%, 80%, or keyword-overlap threshold as an apply rule.

## Tool bridges

Direct product experience, a renamed product, and adjacent tool-family experience are different. A Sentinel user may show relevant detection skills for a Splunk role without claiming SPL. Show the actual product and transferable method, then identify the remaining platform gap. Do not add the requested product to skills solely for search matching.

## Output

| Exact requirement | Importance and timing | Candidate evidence/location | Evidence status | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hands-on AWS or Azure security | Mandatory; screening unknown | “Worked with AWS and Azure security tools” | Claimed only | Clarify the control and personal action; one cloud may satisfy the wording |
| Terraform preferred | Preferred | Skills list: Terraform | Claimed only | Keep the stated skill; ask for an actual example before adding implementation claims |

Give a reasoned application assessment based on responsibilities and confirmed constraints. Missing resume evidence usually calls for clarification or better presentation; it does not automatically require new training or withdrawal.
