# Submission triage: blockers, risks, and unknowns

The legacy filename is retained for compatibility. Do not label ordinary weaknesses “fatal flaws” or predict automatic rejection.

| Class | Evidence needed | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed submission blocker | Direct observation or explicit application instruction | Corrupt file; upload rejects the file; known page limit exceeded; essential text unreadable | Repair the observed problem; continue reviewing recoverable content |
| Confirmed requirement gap | Exact mandatory requirement plus candidate statement that it is unmet | Active clearance required and candidate states none | State the specific mismatch; ask employer about alternatives only where plausible |
| Material risk | Observable weakness with a plausible effect | Interleaved text extraction; unsupported scope claim; vague first role | Prioritize a concrete fix, with uncertainty stated |
| Unknown | Needed evidence unavailable | Only pasted text; missing clearance status; redacted dates | Mark unassessed or ask the relevant question; do not mark failed |
| Optional polish | Style preference without a material defect | Filename aesthetics, restrained accent color | Suggest only if useful |

## Checks

1. **Readable source:** Open the file and inspect extraction. A scanned PDF may be readable through OCR, but OCR results need checking. A local extraction failure is not proof every ATS rejects it.
2. **Contact:** Check usable candidate contact for a standalone submission. Contact intentionally removed for review is not a defect; an application may also collect it separately. Do not mistake test phone numbers for genuine missing contact.
3. **Role and chronology:** Check employer, title, and dates are understandable. Years-only dates may be sufficient for a commercial review; follow explicit month requirements. Overlaps may be concurrent work, consulting, study, or transitions, not deception.
4. **Claims:** Surface concrete contradictions or confirmed inaccuracies. Do not assume a listed credential is expired because an expiry date is absent. Skills need not all be repeated in bullets.
5. **Target eligibility:** Copy the actual requirement before judging it. Separate “currently holds” from “can obtain after hire,” and mandatory from preferred.
6. **Document instructions:** Check actual file type, length, and required fields. Federal guidance has changed; use the federal reference.
7. **Sensitive content:** Remove secrets or restricted operational detail from a public application while preserving approved scope and contribution.

If none are observed, say “No confirmed submission blockers in the material reviewed; original-file parsing [checked / not assessed].” This does not guarantee application acceptance. Keep submission status separate from content score; no arbitrary score caps.
