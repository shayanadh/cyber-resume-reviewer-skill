# Interview stories from established evidence

| Story topic | Exact resume/user evidence | Relevant interview question | Situation/action/result supported so far | Details to prepare |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Actual accomplishment | Source location | Role-specific question | Candidate's contribution; distinguish team actions | Constraints, decisions, validation, lessons; no invented ending |

Useful IT/cyber themes include service recovery, difficult troubleshooting, a control tradeoff, prioritizing exposure, an investigation, remediation after testing, an audit finding, cross-team delivery, developing others, and communicating a risk decision.

Use STAR/CAR as an organizing aid, not a mandate to fabricate each element. Missing details belong in questions. Keep restricted operational and client information out of interview scripts unless approved for disclosure. A story can describe a lesson or an unresolved tradeoff, not just a numerical success.
